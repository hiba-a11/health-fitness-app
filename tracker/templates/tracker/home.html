Hi, I’m Hiva

I struggled with body image for years. Even when I was healthy, I couldn't love my body because everyone around me gaslighted the concept of body positivity. To feel confident, I realized I needed to confront discomfort and actively decide to change.

This website was born from my journey: learning about my anatomy, understanding my body’s needs, and using research to make informed choices about how I want to change. It's a space to empower others to explore their wellness and take control of their bodies, minds, and health.
